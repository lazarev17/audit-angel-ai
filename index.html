How it works

Connect. Clean. Check. Act.

Four steps your team can explain to a Caldicott Guardian in one breath.

01 · Connect

Start from exports you already run

EMIS, SystmOne, Adastra or your care system's CSV. No integration project to begin. Live connections come later, set up by our team.

02 · Clean

Identifiers removed in your environment

A privacy filter running on your side strips names, NHS numbers, addresses and dates. We measure and publish its accuracy for every deployment.

03 · Check

Every record against your standards

NICE, RCGP, UKHSA and the CQC quality statements, plus your own local rules and formularies, applied to 100% of cases.

04 · Act

Findings become tasks, not homework

A prioritised task list, per-clinician feedback and a CQC evidence pack. The AI flags. Your clinicians decide.
