How it works

Connect. Clean. Check. Act.

Four steps your team can explain to a Caldicott Guardian in one breath.

01 · Connect

Start from exports you already run

EMIS, SystmOne, Adastra or your care system's CSV. No integration project to begin. Live connections come later, set up by our team.

02 · Clean

Identifiers removed in your environment

A privacy filter running on your side strips names, NHS numbers, addresses and dates. We measure and publish its accuracy for every deployment.

03 · Check

Every record against your standards

NICE, RCGP, UKHSA and the CQC quality statements, plus your own local rules and formularies, applied to 100% of cases.

04 · Act

Findings become tasks, not homework

A prioritised task list, per-clinician feedback and a CQC evidence pack. The AI flags. Your clinicians decide.

Security, safety & IG

The questions your Caldicott Guardian will ask, answered up front

"Does patient data leave our control?"

Identifiers are removed inside your environment first. The AI checker runs under zero-data-retention terms: nothing stored, nothing used for training. You stay the data controller; we act as processor.

"What if the AI gets it wrong?"

It never decides. Every flag goes to a clinician. Each run includes a clinician-checked sample, and we publish the agreement rate to you.

"Our IT team has no capacity."

Our setup team does the work. The pilot runs from exports you already produce, so there's no integration project to approve.

"IG sign-off will take months."

You get a DPIA template, DTAC pack, clinical safety case and hazard log on day one, ready for your IG and clinical safety leads.

"What if it finds nothing useful?"

The pilot is fixed price with a defined output. If the findings don't justify going further, you keep the report and stop there.

"Is this a medical device?"

Audit Angel supports governance review; clinicians make every clinical decision.
